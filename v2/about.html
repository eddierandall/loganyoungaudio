Logan Young is a producer, guitarist, and songwriter — the artist behind the solo project Dal Av. He built Logan Young Audio to chase tones that are unstable, aggressive, and alive: sounds that spark a riff or an idea the moment you hear them.

"No bloated interfaces. No endless menus. Just focused ideas designed to spark riffs, break habits, and push musicians into unfamiliar territory."

No trend-chasing. No cloning what's already out there — just tools I use myself, built for players who want to push their tone somewhere new.

— LOGAN YOUNG
