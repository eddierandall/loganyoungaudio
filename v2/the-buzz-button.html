THREE FUZZ MODES

PICK YOUR VENOM

DRONE

THE ALL-PURPOSE FUZZ

The most versatile of the three modes. Thick, aggressive, and usable across everything from riffs and chords to leads and breakdowns.

SWARM

DARKER. MEANER. MORE AGGRESSIVE.

A heavier fuzz character with more aggression and a darker overall sound. Built for riffs that need extra bite and weight.

QUEEN

MAXIMUM AGGRESSION

The most extreme fuzz mode in The Buzz Button. Saturated, chaotic, and intentionally over-the-top when you want your guitar tone to completely lose its mind.

INTERNAL ROUTING

THREE WAYS TO BUZZ

Three internal routing modes change how your dry guitar, low octave, and fuzz interact.

I — PARALLEL

Guitar and octave are processed independently before being blended — a clear, controlled fuzz with plenty of low-end weight.

II — SUM

Guitar and octave are blended together before hitting the fuzz, creating a more unified, compressed fuzz character.

III — CASCADE

Guitar and octave are fuzzed independently at reduced drive, blended, then pushed through a second fuzz stage for extra saturation and chaos.
