ABOUT LOGAN

[ PHOTO OF LOGAN ]

Logan Young is a guitarist and producer — a touring member of metalcore band Reflections and the artist behind the solo project Dal Av. Logan Young Audio grew out of his own rig, not a business plan: tools he built because he needed them for his own sessions, made the way he wished plugins actually worked.

Every release is designed to be fast, inspiring, and unapologetically creative — built for musicians who want to break the pattern, not follow it. No trend-chasing, no cloning what already exists: just tools Logan uses himself, for players who want to push their tone into new territory.

"Sounds that felt wrong in the right way — unstable, aggressive, and alive."
